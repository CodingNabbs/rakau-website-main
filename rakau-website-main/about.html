Our mission

To give visitors from all walks of life an immersive, realistic and accessible way to hike through native New Zealand woods, from the comfort of home or the office.

What we offer

We offer a range of virtual hiking trails that cater to different skill levels and interests, allowing users to explore diverse landscapes from the comfort of their homes. Our trails are designed to be engaging, interactive, and accessible, ensuring that users of all abilities can enjoy the experience. Whether you're a seasoned hiker or a beginner, we have something for everyone!
